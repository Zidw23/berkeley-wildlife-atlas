02 / WHERE LIFE HAPPENS

Four worlds, one watershed.

The East Bay is a patchwork of interconnected environments. Each supports a different community of life.

🌲

Redwood forest

Cool, shaded canopies and damp understories create refuge for birds, amphibians, and invertebrates.

🌾

Oak woodland

Native oaks, grasses, and seasonal food sources support a rich network of animals.

🌿

Coastal scrub

Open slopes and dense shrubs provide cover, nesting sites, and pollinator resources.

🌊

Bay wetlands

Tidal marshes and mudflats are essential feeding and resting places for waterbirds.

03 / A DIFFERENT WAY OF SEEING

Nature doesn't stop
at the city line.

Berkeley sits within a landscape shaped by geology, water, climate, and people. Parks, creeks, gardens, shorelines, and neighborhoods are all part of the same ecological story.

FIELD NOTE № 001

Try this: choose one familiar place and spend ten quiet minutes noticing what lives there. Look for movement, listen for calls, and notice where plants meet pavement. Observation is the beginning of understanding.
